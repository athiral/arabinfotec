Your VAT invoice for any monthly subscription or other fees or charges is available for you by Online / on Registered Email Id.

2. The “Monthly Recurring Payment” or “Recurring Payment” means the ARABINFOTEC Monthly / Yearly subscription fee corresponding to the purchased product & services option (e.g. Fixed Term or Flexible), and determined by the subscription type and subscription term at the point of starting the contract.

3. The Monthly / Yearly Subscription fee or Monthly / Yearly Recurring Payment is due each Month / Year in full, upfront, in advance, and with the exception of cancellations under the terms detailed below, no partial refund of the Monthly / Yearly Subscription fee will be made.

4. An “Initial Setup Fee” will be charged upon becoming a subscription member. The Initial Setup Fee is a one-off payment at the point of becoming our customer and is applied to cover the initial administration costs associated with setting up a new SaaS / Any Service Account and recurring payment agreement and entitles you to an initial induction session.

5. During certain special offer periods, the ARABINFOTEC management may in its sole discretion choose to remove or reduce the Initial Setup Fee.

6. The Initial Setup Fee (if one is payable/applicable at the time of you opt our service ) and first Monthly Recurring Payment are collected from you by us either by Debit or Credit card at time of purchase at the same time as you become a customer.

7. Your second Monthly / Yearly Recurring Payment will be collected one Month / Year after you joined. Subsequent Monthly / Yearly Recurring Payments for Monthly / Yearly subscription fees will be collected Monthly / Yearly thereafter.

8. If any Monthly / Yearly Recurring Payment is returned unpaid or any cheque is returned unpaid or if any other form of payment is not honored for whatever reason, you shall pay us on demand an administration fee of AED 150. If despite us having notified you of a missed payment, further payments are missed, we reserve the right to, at our sole election, either suspend or terminate your membership. If you are ever over 30 days in arrears on a missed Monthly / Yearly payment and you are on a Fixed Term subscription then we reserve the right to bill you immediately after the 30 day grace period for the remaining contract value without any prior notification.

9. You are obligated to make the minimum number of Monthly Recurring Payments stated in your choice of subscription. You are obligated to make every Recurring Payment regardless of non-usage, except where the cancellation terms below are met.

10. The date of your Monthly / Yearly recurring Payment is likely to be the same date on each Month / Year as the first Monthly / Yearly Recurring Payment. You are unable to amend the date of your Recurring Payment but we reserve the right to amend the date of your Monthly / Yearly Recurring Payment at our sole discretion.

Once you have completed the minimum number of Monthly / Yearly Recurring Payments as stated in your subscription choice we will automatically continue collecting Monthly / Yearly Recurring Payments every Month/ Year. Your subscription will be extended by one Month / Year for each payment (“Renewal Period”).

CANCELLATION AND REFUNDS

1. If you purchase any of our services online by yourself and not through our sales agent then you have 48 hours to cancel your order and obtain a full refund.

2. Once our technical team initiates the Project and You received a welcome email from our customer care team then you have 24 hours to cancel the order and obtain a partial refund based on the technical resource usage.

3. The Initial Setup Fee & Ont Time Charges is not refundable under any circumstances.

4. Following the above-mentioned period, the initial Setup Fee, One Time payment and each subsequent Monthly Recurring Payment made is not refundable under any circumstances but you may cancel your subscription subject to the service contract.

5. We will manage and process the cancellation of your membership upon receipt of a properly submitted cancellation request. Should your Recurring Payment be canceled without submission of a cancellation request form at any time you may incur fees as a result of any missed payments. If you wish to have confirmation of any amendments to your account at any stage please contact a member of the ARABINFOTECH team.

6. Refunds will be made onto the original mode of payment and will be processed within 45 days depends on the issuing bank of the credit card.

You acknowledge and understand that one further recurring payment will be taken from your account if such a recurring payment is due within the aforementioned 30 days' notice.
